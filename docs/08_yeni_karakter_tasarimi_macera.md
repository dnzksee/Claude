# Yeni yön: Umay ile Zıpzıp, macera çizgi filmi

Seri artık öğretici kısa videolardan çok **maceracı bir animasyon** olacak. Görünüm: yüksek detaylı 3D, sıcak sinematik
ışık (kullanıcının gönderdiği referans gibi). Zıpzıp'ın kanatları var. İleride Umay onun sırtına binecek, birlikte uçarak
maceraya atılacaklar.

## Karakterler (her prompt'ta aynen kullanılır)

**UMAY** (3 yaşında, çok tatlı):
- Kocaman, yuvarlak, ışıl ışıl ela-kahve gözler, uzun kirpikler
- Tombul pembe yanaklar, küçük düğme burun, minik gülümseme
- Kestane rengi **kıvırcık** saç: iki zıplayan kıvırcık kuyruk, sarı çiçek tokalar, alnında kıvırcık perçemler
- Mercan kırmızısı elbise, beyaz yuvarlak yaka, göğsünde küçük sarı kalp, turuncu ayakkabılar
- Macera kıyafeti: küçük ten rengi kaşif şapkası, üstünde pirinç uçuş gözlüğü, minik kahverengi deri sırt çantası

**ZIPZIP** (tatlı, beyaz, kanatlı köpek):
- Pamuk gibi kabarık, bembeyaz tüyler; yuvarlak kafa, kısa burun, parlak siyah düğme burun
- Kocaman parlak koyu gözler, minik pembe dil, yumuşak sarkık kulaklar
- Kırmızı tasma, yuvarlak altın künye
- Sırtında yumuşak, tüylü, **beyaz kanatlar** (uçları hafif altın parıltılı): yürürken katlı, uçarken açık
- Umay'ı sırtında taşıyabilecek kadar büyük ve güçlü, ama hâlâ sevimli bir yavru gibi

**Güvenlik notu (2-5 yaş):** Uçuş sahnelerinde Umay her zaman gözlüklü, iki eliyle Zıpzıp'ın tasmasına tutunur;
uçuş yumuşak ve alçaktan, sihirli ışıltılarla. Böylece "evde atlama/uçma" taklidine özendirmez, sihirli bir hayal olduğu belli olur.

## Adım 1: Karakter kartı (Gemini görsel üretimi)

Önce bir karakter kartı üretilir; sonraki videolarda bu görsel yüklenip "bu karakterleri kullan" denir.
Böylece Umay ile Zıpzıp her klipte aynı görünür.

```
Character design sheet for a 3D animated children's adventure series, premium Pixar / Illumination quality,
soft studio lighting, plain warm cream background. Show each character from the front, three-quarter and side view,
plus a few facial expressions (happy, surprised, giggling).

UMAY: an extremely cute 3-year-old Turkish girl. Very big, round, sparkling hazel-brown eyes with long lashes and
bright catchlights; chubby rosy cheeks; tiny button nose; small sweet smile. Curly chestnut-brown hair: two bouncy
curly pigtails tied with small yellow flower hair ties, soft curly bangs on her forehead. Coral-red short-sleeved
cotton dress with a round white Peter Pan collar and a small yellow heart on the chest; orange shoes. Adventure
accessories: a small tan explorer hat with brass flying goggles on top, and a tiny brown leather backpack.

ZIPZIP: her adorable pure-white puppy with wings. Fluffy cotton-like snow-white fur, round head, short muzzle, shiny
black button nose, big glossy dark eyes, little pink tongue, soft floppy ears, red collar with a round golden tag.
On his back: soft feathery white wings with faint golden shimmer at the tips, shown folded and fully spread.
He is big and strong enough for the girl to ride on his back, but still looks like a sweet, cuddly puppy.

Also show one pose: Umay sitting on Zıpzıp's back holding his collar, both smiling.
```

## Adım 2: Video, macera açılışı (8 sn, dikey)

Karakter kartını Gemini'ye yükle, bu prompt'u yapıştır.

```
Vertical 9:16 video. Premium 3D CGI animated feature-film look, Pixar / Illumination quality. Ultra-detailed tactile
textures: polished wood grain, soft cotton fabric, fluffy white fur, worn leather, brass. Warm cinematic lighting from
glowing round lamps and string lights, soft volumetric sunbeams, big expressive eyes with bright catchlights, shallow
depth of field with creamy bokeh, rich colorful palette.

Use the girl and the winged puppy from the attached character sheet exactly as designed:
UMAY (very cute 3-year-old girl, huge sparkling hazel-brown eyes, curly chestnut pigtails with yellow flower ties,
coral-red dress with white collar and yellow heart, small explorer hat with brass goggles, tiny backpack) and
ZIPZIP (fluffy pure-white puppy, big glossy eyes, red collar with golden tag, soft feathery white wings).

Setting: their cozy "Explorer Treehouse" headquarters inside a big tree: colorful pipes and brass gears on the walls,
glowing lamps, a curved control desk with chunky colorful buttons, a big wooden-framed monitor, a big round window
open to a sunny sky.

Action (8 seconds):
0–2 s: The big monitor flashes with a soft alarm chime and shows a glowing map with a blinking star far away beyond
       the clouds. Umay and Zıpzıp turn toward it, eyes wide.
2–4 s: Close-up: Umay smiles excitedly and says in Turkish, in a sweet toddler voice: "Zıpzıp, macera başlıyor!"
       Zıpzıp barks "Hav!" and slowly spreads his fluffy white wings, golden sparkles floating off the feathers.
4–6 s: Umay pulls her goggles down and climbs onto Zıpzıp's back, holding his collar with both hands, giggling.
6–8 s: Zıpzıp flaps his wings and they glide gently out through the round window into a bright sky with fluffy
       clouds; camera follows them from behind as sunlight sparkles.

Audio: uplifting adventurous orchestral music with soaring strings and glockenspiel, a soft cartoon alarm chime,
whoosh of wings, the girl's happy giggle, one puppy bark. Child-friendly, gentle, nothing scary.
```

## Adım 3 (isteğe bağlı): Uçuş devamı (8 sn, dikey)

```
Same characters and same 3D Pixar-style look as the attached character sheet: UMAY riding on the back of ZIPZIP,
the fluffy white winged puppy; Umay wears her explorer hat with goggles down and holds his red collar with both hands.

They fly gently over a magical sunny landscape: rolling green hills, a sparkling blue river, colorful flower fields,
fluffy clouds they pass right through. Umay laughs with joy, her curly pigtails bouncing in the wind; Zıpzıp's ears
flap, tongue out happily. A flock of little colorful birds flies alongside them. Camera circles slowly around them.
At the end they look ahead and see a glowing golden star shining on top of a distant cloud castle.

Audio: soaring joyful orchestral music, wind whoosh, the girl's delighted "Vuuu!", happy puppy bark. Child-friendly.
```

## İpuçları

- Dikey çıkmazsa Gemini'de video ayarlarından 9:16 seçilir.
- Karakter kartı beğenilmezse "make her eyes even bigger", "make her curls softer" gibi tek cümlelik düzeltmelerle
  yeniden üretilir. Beğenilen kart bundan sonraki bütün kliplerde kullanılır.
- Kanal logosu ve banner'ı da yeni tasarıma (kıvırcık saçlı Umay, kanatlı beyaz Zıpzıp) göre yenilenecek.
